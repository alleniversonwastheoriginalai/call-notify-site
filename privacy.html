Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Call App ("Call App", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information in the Call Out mobile app and its phone calls and text messages (together, the "Service").

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Information we collect

From people who use the app

  • Phone number. Used to sign you in with a one-time text code and as the only number the Service calls on your behalf.
  • Name. The name you choose, which your emergency contacts hear if you miss a safety check-in.
  • Call plans. The calls you schedule: times, how often to retry, the message you typed, and whether to alert contacts.
  • Voice notes. Messages you record over a phone call, stored so they can be played on your calls.
  • Emergency contacts. The names and phone numbers you add, and whether each person has agreed to be your contact.
  • Location, only if you turn it on. While a safety check-in with location sharing is active, the app keeps your most recent location on our servers. We store only your latest location, never a history of where you have been.
  • Call and message records. When calls and texts were placed, and whether they were answered.

From emergency contacts

  • Your name and phone number, as entered by the person who invited you.
  • Your opt-in: the time you texted YES to agree, or STOP to opt out.

How we use information

  • To place the calls and send the texts you schedule.
  • If you miss a safety check-in you set up, to alert the emergency contacts who agreed to it, including your message and, if you turned it on, your last known location.
  • To send an all-clear text to those contacts when you mark yourself safe.
  • To prevent abuse, such as calling numbers that have not agreed to receive messages.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers solely to operate the Service: Twilio, to place calls and send texts, and Supabase, to host our database and servers. They process information on our behalf and may not use it for their own marketing. Twilio's copy of a voice-note recording is deleted once it is transferred to our storage.

SMS messaging

Emergency contacts receive text messages from Call Out only after they opt in themselves by texting YES and their invite code to our number. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. See our SMS Terms and how opt-in works.

Retention and deletion

  • Your location is overwritten each time it updates, and is no longer collected once no check-in needs it.
  • You can delete call plans, contacts and voice notes in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app (You → Delete account). This permanently deletes your account and all associated data.
  • An emergency contact can text STOP at any time to stop all messages and calls.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to each person's data is restricted to that person by database access rules. Calls and texts can only be placed by our servers, and our telephony credentials are never stored in the app.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
