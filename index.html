Real phone calls, right when you need them

Call Out calls you at a time you choose: to wake you up, remind you, give you a way out of a bad date, or check that you're safe. If you miss a safety check-in, it alerts the emergency contacts who agreed to it, with your message and last known location.

Call Out is not an emergency service and never calls 911. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
